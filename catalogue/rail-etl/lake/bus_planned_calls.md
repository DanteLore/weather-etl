# lake.bus_planned_calls — Bus timetable by day, Thatcham area

## Description

The bus timetable in the Thatcham area, day by day: one row per timetabled journey per stop per day, for every stop inside the area (the A4 corridor from Newbury to Theale). Each row has the operator, line, journey, its first and last stops, the stop, and the timetabled arrival and departure in UK local time. Built from each morning's GTFS snapshot, so each day's rows are the timetable as published that day. Use it for bus frequency by stop and hour, the first and last buses, and as the plan that [`lake.bus_service_calls`](bus_service_calls.md) measures against.

Built from [`incoming.bods_gtfs_*`](../incoming/bods_gtfs.md).

## Common queries

Buses per hour from Thatcham Broadway (both directions), with the lines:

```sql
select scheduled_hour, count(*) as departures, array_join(array_agg(distinct line), ', ') as lines
from lake.bus_planned_calls
where service_date = date '2026-11-12' and stop_name = 'Thatcham Broadway' and not is_destination
group by 1 order by 1
```

On Thursday 2026-11-12 that gave 4 to 7 buses an hour from 07:00 to 18:00 on lines 1, 1a and 1c, and the last bus (a Jet Black 1) in the 21:00 hour.

## Known issues & caveats

- **The published timetable, not what ran.** For what ran, see [`lake.bus_service_calls`](bus_service_calls.md).
- Only stops inside the area; a journey from Newbury to Reading has rows for Newbury, Thatcham and the villages to Theale but not Reading.
- Days before 2026-11-12 aren't available, and only days with a snapshot are covered (a missed snapshot leaves a gap).
- `trip_id` is generated by BODS and changes between snapshots; to follow a journey across days use operator, line, origin stop and departure time.
- Times are UK local, as GTFS gives them. Journeys after midnight belong to the previous day's `service_date`.
- Demand-responsive services (West Berkshire's DRT) have no timetable.
